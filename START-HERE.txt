FIX
Direct edits preserve the glyph-to-Unicode mappings of the actual text.
The prepared text layer is saved/reopened and checked before source removal.
This prevents edited subset-font text reopening as glyph codes or gibberish.
It also fixes the shared Find and replace and native move/duplicate paths.
Original fonts, styles and placement are retained when supported.
Other text and spreadsheet/workspace functions have been regression checked.

EXISTING CORRUPTED PDFs
Use Undo/History or reopen the unmodified original, then reapply your edit
with this build. This fix does not guess at characters already lost from a PDF.

Full source kits, licences, notices and MuPDF corresponding-source parts
are included. See README.md, RELEASE-NOTES.md and SOURCE-COMMIT.txt.
All processing remains on this device; built-in engines need no network.
