PDFCreator — inline editing and optional helper bar — 1 November 2026

Open index.html in a current desktop browser. pdfcreator.html and the legacy
pdfpro.html are identical runnable aliases. Processing stays on this device.

Direct edit shows formatting in the editable text box. Font, size, colour and
emphasis controls appear at the top. Select words to format them, or leave the
selection empty to format the whole block. Apply saves the edit to the PDF.

Hide the helper bar with its × button. Restore it using the top three-dot menu
and Show helper bar. This preference is remembered on this device.

The embedded-font Unicode preservation fix and XLSX/CSV form generation remain
included. Previously corrupted PDFs still require Undo/History or an original
copy; this release does not guess at characters already lost from a PDF.
